The Electricity Industry: How Power Actually Moves

A field guide to the value chain - generation, transmission, distribution, retail - and the structural tensions that decide who profits, who pays, and what gets built next.

April 2026

Electricity is the most consequential commodity in the modern economy - and most people don't stop to question the infrastructure that makes it all possible. It cannot be stored at scale, must be produced at the same instant it is consumed, and travels through a physical network that took a century to build and cannot be rerouted in a hurry. Every electron a household uses has passed through four distinct businesses on its way to the kettle. This page walks the chain.

Where the arguments are live

An industry that feels static is, structurally, in the middle of its biggest reorganisation since the post-war buildout. Four open dossiers - each is also a political fight.

✦

The structural read: The electricity industry is not one business - it is four. Each segment has different capital intensity, different regulatory exposure, different competitive logic. Confusing them produces almost every bad take on energy policy.
