About

Hi, I'm Anthony — a writer and consultant based in Auckland, New Zealand.

My brain works in systems. I'm drawn to the way institutions resist or adapt to technological change, how the regulation being written today shapes what's commercially viable tomorrow, and how the same AI capability looks like an opportunity from one angle and a governance challenge from another. I find complexity most interesting when it can't be neatly assigned to any one discipline.

I care most about AI — not just what it can do, but who controls the infrastructure it runs on, what it means for institutional power at scale, and whether responsible governance can actually produce better outcomes for people. These feel like the most important questions of the next few decades, and they're the ones I'm building my career around.

My goal is to find the non-obvious angle and make it legible — for organisations, for decisions, and for people trying to navigate complexity. This site is where I publish that work — through Considered, my long-form writing, and Macro, an interactive library of explainers on the systems that shape the world.

Background

I'm currently finishing a BCom in Information Systems at the University of Auckland, including a capstone project on AI procurement strategy in the public sector for Microsoft NZ.

I've spent three years at Nui Markets, an agri-commodities tech startup, where I currently own AI process design and implementation. I've always been drawn to the startup world and working inside one has given me a grounding in the commercial reality that sits behind the bigger ideas I write about.

In early 2027, I'll be joining Deloitte NZ in Tech, Strategy & Transformation. Before that, I'm spending five months travelling across Europe and Asia — structured around cultural immersion and scoping cities I might live and work in later.

Outside of work I enjoy playing badminton, choral singing, piano, and bouldering; if you share any of these interests, reach out — I'm always up for a good chat!
